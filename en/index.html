Frequently asked questions

How much will it make me?

We don't promise a number: it depends on your product, your customers and your ads. What we promise: your 5 numbers before we start, the same numbers every month after, and an honest way to see what actually comes from us.

Why do results take time?

Because the reminder goes out when your product runs out. If a jar lasts 30 days, the first repeat order comes around 30 days after purchase. That's why we measure month after month, not in the first week.

Do I have to commit for long?

No. Your store analysis is free, with no commitment. The Build is paid once, and only if it's needed. Monthly plans have no commitment, month to month: you can stop at the end of any month. You can switch plans from one month to the next.

Which plan should I choose?

Foundation if you already have good basics and want them maintained and improved. Growth if you want to actively grow your retention: it's the one we recommend to most stores. Partner if you want to hand off the strategy and almost all of the execution, SMS included. We'll make a recommendation on the call, based on what we see in your store.

Do I have to pay for the Build?

Only if your system needs it. If your automations and segmentation are already well developed, we go straight to monthly management. The free analysis is how we find out.

How is this different from an email agency?

An email agency delivers emails. We take on your retention strategy: we start from your numbers, manage your automations, campaigns and segmentation, measure your second-order rate every month and improve what needs improving. We also tell you when the problem is elsewhere, like your offer or shipping.

I already have automated emails.

Great, we start from there. The analysis shows what already brings your customers back, what to fix and what's missing. If your system is developed enough, there's no Build: we go straight to management.

I could do this myself.

You could, the tools are there. Do you know on what day your customers reorder your product? That's what we find first. And how long has it been on your to-do list?

I already have a loyalty app.

Points don't do much if nobody reminds the customer to come back. We can bring it up at the right moment, so it actually gets used.

What tools do you work with?

We work in your Shopify store and in your Klaviyo account, which is how we reach your customers by email and SMS. If you don't have Klaviyo yet, we'll talk about it on the call.

What happens to my customers' data?

We work with a user account created for us, never your passwords. We sign a confidentiality agreement, and access is removed when we stop working together.

Does what we send your customers comply with anti-spam law?

Every email and SMS includes the information CASL requires: your business name, contact information (including your mailing address, directly or through a link) and a way to unsubscribe. You remain responsible for your list's consent. We flag anything that looks doubtful.

Do you also do ads or websites?

No. We handle what happens after the first purchase: bringing your customer back. No ads, no website redesign, no untargeted newsletters blasted to your whole list.
