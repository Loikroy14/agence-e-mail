Frequently asked questions

How much will it make me?

We don't promise a number: it depends on your product, your list and your traffic. Before the call, we can show you what happens today after someone buys from you. With the Complete package, you get a 60-day report with the sales Klaviyo attributes to these emails.

Why do results take time?

Because the reminder goes out when your product runs out. If a jar lasts 30 days, the first repeat order comes around 30 days after purchase. Welcome and abandoned-cart emails start running as soon as they're turned on.

I already have a loyalty app.

Points don't do much if nobody reminds the customer to come back. Our emails can put your program front and centre.

I could do this myself.

You could. How long has it been on your to-do list? We deliver in 7 days, in both languages, tested.

Do I need Klaviyo?

Yes. We work in your Klaviyo account connected to Shopify. If you don't have one yet, we'll talk about it on the call.

What happens to my customers' data?

We work with a user account created for us, never your passwords. We sign a confidentiality agreement, and access is removed at the end of the project.

Will my emails comply with anti-spam law?

Every email includes the information CASL requires: your business name, mailing address, a way to reach you and an unsubscribe link. You remain responsible for your list's consent. We flag anything that looks doubtful.

Do you also do newsletters, SMS or ads?

No. We only do the automated emails that bring your customers back.
