Questions

What people ask us.

How much will it make me?

We don't promise a number: it depends on your product, your list and your traffic. What we can do before the call is show you what happens today after someone buys from you. And with the Complete package, you get a 60-day report with the sales Klaviyo attributes to these emails.

Why do results take time?

Because the reminder goes out when your product runs out. If a jar lasts 30 days, the first repeat order comes around 30 days after purchase. Welcome and abandoned-cart emails start working in the first week.

I already have a loyalty app.

Great. Points don't do much if nobody reminds the customer to come back. Our emails put your app to work.

I could do this myself.

You could. How many months has it been on your to-do list? We get it done in 7 days, in both languages, tested and compliant.

Do I need Klaviyo?

Yes, we work in your Klaviyo account connected to Shopify. If you don't have one yet, we'll talk about it on the call.

What happens to my customer data?

We work with a separate user account, never your passwords. Access is removed at the end of the project, and we sign a confidentiality agreement.

Why English and French?

Because your customers are all over Canada. Quebec customers expect to be served in French, and the rest of the country in English. Every customer gets the email in their language, written for them, not translated.

How much of my time does it take?

Very little: the 20-minute call, the questionnaire and the review. We handle the rest.

Do you also do newsletters, SMS or ads?

No. We only do the automated emails that bring your customers back. It's our specialty, and that's why we do it well.
