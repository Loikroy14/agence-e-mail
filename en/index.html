Frequently asked questions

How much will it make me?

We don't promise a number: it depends on your product, your customers and your ads. What we promise: your 5 numbers before we start, the same numbers every month after, and an honest way to see what actually comes from us.

Why do results take time?

Because the reminder goes out when your product runs out. If a jar lasts 30 days, the first repeat order comes around 30 days after purchase. That's why we measure month after month, not in the first week.

Do I have to commit for long?

No. Your store analysis is free and the setup is paid once, with no required subscription. Care is month to month, and you only choose it after the 30-day results call. Management asks for 3 months at the start, long enough for repeat orders to show in your numbers, then it's month to month.

I already have automated emails.

Great, we start from there. The analysis shows what to fix and what's missing, no need to redo everything.

I could do this myself.

You could, the tools are there. Do you know on what day your customers reorder your product? That's what we find first. And how long has it been on your to-do list?

I already have a loyalty app.

Points don't do much if nobody reminds the customer to come back. Our emails and campaigns can put your program front and centre.

Do I need Klaviyo?

Yes. We work in your Klaviyo account connected to Shopify. If you don't have one yet, we'll talk about it on the call.

What happens to my customers' data?

We work with a user account created for us, never your passwords. We sign a confidentiality agreement, and access is removed when we stop working together.

Will my emails comply with anti-spam law?

Every email includes the information CASL requires: your business name, mailing address, a way to reach you and an unsubscribe link. You remain responsible for your list's consent. We flag anything that looks doubtful.

Do you also do SMS or ads?

No. We only handle what brings your customer back after their purchase. Our campaigns are targeted by customer group, not newsletters sent to your whole list.
