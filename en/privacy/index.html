Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Revendo is the business name of Loïk Roy, in Quebec. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to revendo.ca and to our exchanges with businesses that contact us.

Person in charge of protecting personal information

Loïk Roy, founder
info@revendo.ca

Contact this person with any question, access or correction request, or complaint.

What we collect

  • When you book a call: your name, email, store name and address, and anything you choose to write to us.
  • When you write to us: your name, email and the content of your message.
  • When you become a client: the information needed for billing, and the access you give us to your Klaviyo and Shopify accounts.

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no ad tracking. Fonts are hosted on our own site, with no calls to third-party services. Our web host may keep connection logs (IP address, date, page requested) for technical and security reasons.

Why we use it

  • To set up the call you requested and follow up on it.
  • To send you a proposal and, if you become a client, do the work and bill you.
  • To meet our legal obligations (accounting, taxes).

We never sell or rent personal information. We never add you to a mailing list without your consent.

Our clients' customer data

When a store gives us access to its Klaviyo or Shopify account, we may see information about its own customers. We then act only on the store's behalf and according to its instructions. We use a dedicated user account, never the store's passwords. Everyone who works on the project signs a confidentiality agreement. Our access is removed at the end of the engagement. Order exports used for the analysis are deleted at that point, and we keep no copy of that data.

Who we share it with

  • Booking tool: Cal.com, to manage appointments.
  • Video calls: Zoom, for meetings.
  • Email: Zoho Mail, with data hosted in Canada.
  • Collaborators: the specialists who work with us on a project, under a confidentiality agreement, and only for that project.
  • Payments and accounting: Stripe for card payments, and our financial institution for Interac transfers.

Some of these providers or collaborators may store or access information outside Quebec or Canada (notably in the United States, for Cal.com, Zoom and Stripe). Before doing so, we assess whether the information will be adequately protected there, and we put a written agreement in place.

How long we keep it

Information about a prospect who doesn't become a client is deleted after 12 months without contact. Client information is kept for the length of the engagement, then as long as tax law requires (generally 6 years for accounting records).

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to withdraw your consent. Write to the person named above. We reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

We protect information with reasonable measures: accounts secured with two-factor authentication, access limited to the people who need it, and access removed at the end of every project. If an incident poses a risk of serious harm, we notify the people affected and the authorities, as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with its update date.
